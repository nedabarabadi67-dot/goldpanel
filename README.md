# GoldPanel – Advanced Business Management & CRM System

A full-stack web application built with Python and Django.
GoldPanel is a comprehensive business management system designed for real-world business operations including CRM, sales tracking, accounting, inventory management, and SMS marketing for gold and jewelry businesses.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

GoldPanel is a modular and scalable management system that integrates multiple business modules into a single platform.
It is designed to manage customers, sales, financial records, inventory (gold products), and marketing campaigns efficiently.

This project simulates a real-world enterprise system and demonstrates full-stack development capabilities.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👥 CRM (Customer Relationship Management)

* Customer database management
* Customer segmentation and categorization
* Purchase history tracking
* Customer profile management

---

### 📢 Marketing & SMS Campaign System

* Create marketing campaigns
* Send SMS to selected customer groups
* Filter customers based on behavior and purchases
* Targeted SMS automation system

---

### 📊 Sales Analytics & Reporting

* Sales dashboard with charts and analytics
* Revenue tracking and performance reports
* Product and customer sales statistics
* Business insights visualization

---

### 💰 Accounting System

* Basic accounting module
* Income and expense tracking
* Financial summaries and reporting
* Integration with sales system

---

### 🪙 Gold Inventory Management

* Management of gold bars (Aabshodeh)
* Stock tracking and valuation
* Inventory control system
* Integration with sales and accounting

---

### 📦 System Features

* User authentication system
* Role-based access control (Admin / Staff)
* Admin dashboard
* Full CRUD functionality
* Responsive UI design with Bootstrap
